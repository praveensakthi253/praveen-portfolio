Privacy & credits

Your privacy, and who made what.

This site is a personal portfolio. It doesn't track you, and it doesn't collect anything about you unless you choose to contact me.

Privacy notice

In short: no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies.

Who is responsible

Praveen Sakthi, an independent freelancer. You can reach me at my email address.

What this site stores on your device

One thing, in your browser's local storage: your light or dark mode choice (key praveen-mode). It is not a cookie and it never leaves your device. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.

When you contact me

The "Hire me" form doesn't send anything by itself. It opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send there. I use what you send only to reply to you and discuss your project, and I'll delete it if you ask.

The hosting provider

Like every website, this one is delivered by a hosting company, whose servers briefly process technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages. I don't use those logs to identify visitors.
